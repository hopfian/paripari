# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are here to study a catalog. Be decent to each other while doing it.

## The short version

- Be respectful, patient, and assume good faith.
- Critique code, data, and claims — never people.
- No harassment, bigotry, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Keep discussions about the project; leave politics-flaming and advertising at the door.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainer (see `CITATION.cff` for contact).
Maintainers may edit, delete, or reject content and ban repeat offenders to keep the
community healthy.

## Attribution

This code of conduct adapts the standard norms of the Contributor Covenant family without
reproducing it verbatim. When in doubt, be kinder than required.
